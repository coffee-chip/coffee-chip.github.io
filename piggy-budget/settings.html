How the buckets work

Each new day adds 1/30 of the monthly allocation. A bucket can hold up to three months of its current allocation. Purchases subtract from its balance; you can record a purchase even if it takes the balance below zero. Future daily additions bring it back up.

Starting funds can be 0, 1, 2, or 3 months. Changing an allocation affects future daily additions; it does not rewrite earlier spending or additions. The new three-month cap applies right away.
